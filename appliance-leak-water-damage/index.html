Appliance Leak Water Damage in Addison, TX

A failed washer hose, dishwasher seal or ice maker line can soak floors and walls fast, and we extract and dry what the leak reached.

☎ (972) 945-2885

Appliance leak water damage starts when a washer, dishwasher, refrigerator or disposal lets water out where it was never meant to go. Our 24/7 line is open, so you can call at any hour and we will talk you through what to do next.

While you wait, unplug the appliance only if the floor around it is dry, and close its supply valve. If water is near an outlet or you cannot tell, leave the power alone and shut the breaker for that room from the panel.

Why the failed appliance changes the damage

A leak is not just a wet floor. Where the water came from tells us how far it traveled, how clean it was and how long it sat. A burst supply hose under pressure floods a room in minutes. A tired door seal can drip for weeks and leave a rotten subfloor behind.

In North Texas homes the laundry closet, the dishwasher alcove and the fridge nook are often tucked against interior walls and cabinets. Water that escapes there runs under flooring and into wall bases where you cannot see it.

Washing machines

Supply hoses, drain hoses and overflow from a clogged standpipe are the usual culprits. If your laundry room is upstairs, water can drop through the ceiling below. In a condo or apartment building, it may reach a neighbor's unit too.

Dishwashers, fridges and disposals

A dishwasher can leak at the door seal, the drain line or the inlet valve. A refrigerator or ice maker line drips behind the unit for days before anyone sees it. A disposal leaks from its seals and drain connections into the sink base.

First-hour safety steps

Do these in order, and stop at any step that feels unsafe.

  • Close the supply valve behind or beside the appliance. If it will not turn, shut the home's main water valve instead.
  • Keep out of standing water if any cord, outlet or power strip is wet. Switch off the room's breaker from a dry spot.
  • If your inside valve is stuck, call the Town of Addison. Only an authorized agent may work the water meter.
  • Call 911 for sparking, a burning smell or anyone shocked. Call the gas utility if you smell gas near a gas dryer or range.
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics to dry ground and take photos before you touch anything else.

Which water is it

A clean supply line leaks water that started clean. A dishwasher or washer drain carries food, soap, lint and bacteria, so that water is treated as contaminated. We sort this out first because it decides whether carpet pad, baseboard and drywall can be dried or have to come out.

Our process after an appliance leak

We follow industry practice under ANSI/IICRC S500, and we start with measuring.

  1. Find the wet area. We use moisture meters and a thermal camera to map how far the water went past what you can see.
  2. Extract. Portable extractors pull standing water and soaked carpet pad out. Bigger spills get a truck-mount unit.
  3. Open what cannot dry. We pull toe kicks and baseboards, and cut wet drywall or flooring when drying in place would not work.
  4. Dry. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run until readings match a dry reference. Wall cavities can get cavity drying.
  5. Clean and document. We clean affected surfaces, treat contaminated areas and record readings and photos for your adjuster.

If the water reached a cabinet run, our kitchen water damage page explains that side. A failed tank is a different job, covered under water heater leak cleanup.

How insurance often views appliance leaks

Coverage wording varies, so your agent or adjuster has the final word. Many policies treat a sudden hose burst differently from a slow leak that went on for a long time. Wear, neglect and gradual seepage are often handled separately from a sudden break.

Take dated photos of the appliance, the source and the damage before cleanup. Keep any failed hose or part if you can. Our written moisture readings give your adjuster a record of what was wet and when.

What this means in Addison

The Town of Addison dispatches an operator for an emergency water turn-off with no charge, and Town crews are available 24 hours. Only an authorized agent may turn the meter on or off.

Plumbing work such as replacing water lines needs a permit from Development Services, but replacing a faucet or fixture does not. Drywall and flooring replacement are not spelled out, so ask Building Inspection.

Addison buys its water from the City of Dallas, and the 2023 water quality report lists chloramines as the disinfectant. Disinfected supply water is still not safe to leave in a structure, because it feeds mold once materials stay wet.

Mistakes to avoid

These small habits cause most repeat damage.

  • Pushing the appliance back into place over wet flooring.
  • Running a household fan and calling it dry.
  • Skipping the hose check on an old washer.
  • Leaving the supply valve open while you are away.

Replace rubber hoses on a schedule, and check for a damp floor each month. If water has spread toward the walls, our water damage cleanup page shows the full job. We serve neighbors in Carrollton and Plano the same way.
