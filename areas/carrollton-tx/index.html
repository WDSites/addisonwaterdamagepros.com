When water is spreading in Carrollton

Start at the source. Close the fixture shutoff, or the main valve if you cannot find the fixture valve, then move valuables above the wet line and photograph the damage.

Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. If water is reaching outlets or sewage is involved, stay out and call 911 or the utility before you call us.

Then call the 24/7 line and describe the water: where it started, how many rooms are wet and how long it has been running. A same-day response is offered when crews are available, and the person on the line will tell you what to do while you wait.

Check Carrollton's floodplain lookup before the next storm

The City of Carrollton participates in the National Flood Insurance Program and the Community Rating System. Its Engineering page offers FEMA flood map panels by county, a street-level floodplain map and a parcel map that shows whether a lot sits in a floodplain.

Use it before you need it. Texas Department of Insurance guidance is that home policies do not cover floods, so flood damage needs a separate flood policy, and the city's lookup tells you whether to ask your insurer about one.

The FEMA Flood Map Service Center gives the same answer for any address if the city page is slow.

Permits after a plumbing failure

The city's Building Inspection department says permits are required for repair or replacement of the plumbing, mechanical and electrical systems of a building, and that general maintenance does not need one. Cosmetic work such as paint, carpet, cabinets and trim needs no permit.

The city's page does not name water heaters, so ask Building Inspection before you replace one after a leak. We handle the drying and cleanup, and your plumber handles the permit for any plumbing replacement.

What we do for Carrollton homes

A typical Carrollton loss starts with emergency water removal if water is standing, followed by water mitigation to dry walls and floors with air movers and dehumidifiers. Once readings meet drying goals, water damage repair rebuilds drywall, flooring and trim.

If the water was contaminated or ran for days, water damage cleanup removes wet materials and sanitizes surfaces. Scope and price are explained before work starts, and every job gets moisture readings and photos.

Crews also serve neighboring Coppell and Richardson from the same dispatch line.

If water is still spreading: before we arrive

Four steps buy you time while crews are on the way.

Stop the source

Shut the fixture valve or the main. If water is coming through a ceiling, shut off the water to the floor above.

Cut power safely

Switch off breakers for wet areas only from a dry spot. Never touch a panel while standing in water.

Lift and photograph

Raise furniture legs onto foil or blocks, lift rugs and electronics, and take photos before moving anything.

Call the 24/7 line

Tell the dispatcher the source, the rooms affected and whether the water looks clean, gray or like sewage.
