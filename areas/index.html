Local detail for every city

Each area page explains the housing and weather that shape water damage there. Carrollton covers older neighborhoods and winter pipe freezes, Richardson covers slab leaks and aging supply lines, Plano covers seasonal swings and attic leaks, and Coppell covers creek-side lots and drainage.

Each page also lists the ZIP codes in that city, how to reach dispatch, and the first steps to take while you wait. Pick your city for details, or call if you are unsure which one fits.

Addison itself is the home base, so calls from Addison addresses are the easiest to place. For neighboring cities, the dispatcher confirms your address and cross street first, then sends the crew whose route to you is shortest.

Water damage does not follow city lines, so a loss that starts in one city can reach a neighbor's wall or yard. If water is crossing a shared wall in a duplex, townhome or apartment, tell the dispatcher so the other unit can be checked.
