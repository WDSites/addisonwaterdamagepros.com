Water Damage Services in Addison, TX

Six services, one 24/7 dispatch line, and a written scope before work starts.

☎ (972) 945-2885

Addison Water Damage Pros handles water damage in the order it happens. The first call is usually about stopping and removing water, so emergency water removal comes first, followed by water mitigation to dry the structure before damage spreads.

Once the source is stopped, water damage cleanup removes wet materials and sanitizes what remains. When the structure reads dry, water damage repair rebuilds drywall, flooring and finishes.

Storm water, creek overflow and sewage backups call for flood damage restoration, which adds safety steps for contaminated water. Mold is a secondary service, and mold remediation covers containment, removal and testing questions after a leak.

Every service starts with an inspection and moisture readings, and the scope and price are explained before work begins. Dispatch is open 24/7, and the same line covers Addison, Carrollton, Richardson, Plano and Coppell.

How to choose the right service

If water is still on the floor, start with emergency water removal. If the water is gone but walls, carpet or subfloor feel damp, you need water mitigation to dry them before mold or swelling starts.

If damaged materials need to come out and the space needs cleaning, water damage cleanup is the next step. After everything reads dry, water damage repair puts the room back together.
