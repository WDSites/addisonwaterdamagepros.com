Why is my carpet or wall still wet after I mopped and ran a fan?

Because the surface is only the part you can see. Water wicks into carpet pad, behind baseboards, up drywall and down into the subfloor, and a household fan moves air across the top of all that. The material feels dry in a day while the cavity behind it stays damp.

That hidden moisture is what water mitigation deals with. Mitigation is the set of steps that stop the damage from spreading: finding and shutting off the source, extracting standing water, mapping where moisture went, and setting up controlled drying. Texas Department of Insurance guidance notes mold can start growing within a day after a leak, so the first days count.

What happens first on an emergency call

Every job follows the same order, though the details change with the home.

  1. Shut-off and safety check. The crew confirms the water is off at a fixture valve or the main, looks for electrical and slip hazards, and decides which areas are safe to enter.
  2. Extraction. Truck-mounted or portable extraction units pull standing water, and a weighted wand pulls water out of carpet and pad.
  3. Moisture mapping. A moisture meter and a thermal camera trace water through walls, floors and ceilings. The readings and photos become your drying record.
  4. Drying setup. Air movers, dehumidifiers and sometimes injection or cavity drying are placed based on the map, and wet material that cannot dry is removed.

Structural drying

Structural drying is the longest part of the job. Air movers push dry air across wet surfaces and into wall cavities, and low grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of that air and drain it away. In very damp or cold conditions a crew may add desiccant dehumidification. Wall cavities are dried by drilling small vent holes or lifting baseboards, and floor systems can be dried through the subfloor with mats or ports.

Each material has a drying goal, which is the moisture reading it reaches when it is dry for that building. A technician sets those goals on day one using a dry reference reading from an unaffected part of the home, then checks the equipment and the readings every day. Equipment gets moved, added or pulled as the readings change.

Drying times depend on the water category, the materials, humidity, and how far water spread. Many Category 1 jobs finish in about 3 to 5 days, while thick wood floors, plaster, and sewage cleanup can run a week or longer. Equipment stays until readings meet the drying goals, then it is removed. A home that feels dry may not be, which is why the meter decides, not the calendar.

Water category, what it means, and what is done

Industry practice under ANSI/IICRC S500 sorts water by contamination. Ask any company whether its crews work to that standard.

Water categoryWhat it meansWhat is done
Category 1Clean source, like a supply line, faucet or melted ice. It can degrade within a day or two.Extract, dry in place, and save most carpet, pad and drywall.
Category 2Contaminated, like washer or dishwasher discharge or a sump failure. May cause illness if swallowed.Extract, remove and replace pad and porous finishes, clean and dry the rest.
Category 3Grossly contaminated, like sewage or rising floodwater.Remove porous materials, clean and sanitize surfaces, dry the structure. Keep people out.

Clean water that sits for days can move into a higher category.

Safety: when to stay out and call 911 first

Water and electricity do not mix. Stay out of the room if you see any of these, and call 911 or the utility before you call us.

  • Water touching an electrical panel, outlet or a running appliance.
  • Sagging ceiling with water pooling above light fixtures.
  • Sewage in the water, or a strong gas smell.
  • Cut power only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water.

If the Town of Addison water meter is leaking, Town utilities say an operator will be dispatched immediately and there is no charge for water turn-off services. Only an authorized agent may turn water on or off at the meter, and the plumber who repairs the pipe should turn it back on.

Freezing weather and thawing pipes

Frozen pipes often split without leaking until they thaw. NWS Fort Worth recorded two stretches at or below freezing of 93 hours and 139 hours during the February 2021 winter event, which is why many homes found water only after the ice melted. If a pipe has frozen, know where your main shut-off is before it thaws.

Town of Addison advice is to turn sprinklers off when a freeze is forecast, and the Town can shut off water on an emergency basis for a leak or burst pipe.
